Geolocation

My audience is primarily business owners and managers in southern Utah. They are in places like Toquerville, Hurricane, and LaVerkin.

Age

The age of my audience is adults. People old enough to be hiring someone for a job.

Gender

There isn't really a higher rate of either men or women that would be looking at my resume site.

Preferences

The people who would prefer to hire me are those who like a clean, moral, and friendly worker. People who might look at my resume might prefer younger workers, someone with clean language, and a cheerful attitude.
