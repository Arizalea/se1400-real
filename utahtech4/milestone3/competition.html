Their Pros

Something positive the competition has is experience. They might have had several jobs, and know their line of work. They also might be willing to work more hours because they have more time in the day.

Their Cons

The competition may have more experience, but maybe they have a weak resume because their past bosses or clients were unhappy with their work. The competition can have negative attitudes, and make the working environment undesirable.

How I Can Beat That

Because so much of my day is already planned, it is unlikely that I would be late to work. Unexpected occurences are rare. Being on time is something I care a lot about. Also, I always try to have a good attitude when speaking to anyone. I have a lot of people's good opinions on my side, from Youth Leaders to Family to Friends. I can prove that people trust me and even enjoy my company.
