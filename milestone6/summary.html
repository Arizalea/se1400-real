The Silver Bracelet

Emma Stone, a thirteen-year-old girl, unexpectedly becomes the bearer of a powerful, mysterious silver bracelet with dimension-altering capabilities. This bracelet, imbued with an artificial intelligence named Carol, chooses Emma to save multiple dimensions from destruction caused by an antagonist known as the Doctor (Derrick). After being transported from her familiar world to a utopian dimension called Perfection, Emma must learn to harness the bracelet’s powers to prevent cosmic chaos initiated by the Doctor’s attempts to manipulate reality to resurrect his deceased wife, Allyssa.

 

Selene and the Temple of Counfounded Luck or Incredible Misfortune

Selene is cursed. Every night she transforms into a fox, and the curse will only be worse when she turns 18. Desperate to end the trial that has plagued her family for decades, she enlists the help of a comical old antique collecter and his handsome nephew. Together they must navigate the perils of the jungle and learn the secrets of Selene's family before it is too late.

 

My Mask Aside

Aysia, a hardworking young woman born into a poor farming family, lives under the crushing expectation that she must secure a better future for her loved ones through a strategic marriage rather than love. When she enters a royal competition to win the hand of Prince Jared, she navigates the deceptive world of nobility while secretly forming a genuine connection with him—unaware that he is disguising himself as a gardener. As their bond deepens through shared values and emotional understanding, Aysia is abducted by the Enchantress Elcina and discovers her own powerful magic, fueled by emotion and tied to love, trust, fear, and grief. Meanwhile, Jared risks everything to find her, growing from a reluctant prince into a determined leader. Through trials of manipulation, emotional awakening, and magical discovery, both must confront darkness within and around them in order to reunite and determine whether love, trust, and identity can survive in a world built on masks.

 

Skyborn- The Path to the Sky

Ezra, an average, or even under-performing teenage boy is restored a power he didn't know was his in the first place. When he suddenly recieves the ability to fly, he is invited to attent a prestigious school for those who are "chosen by the sky". There, with new friends, he discovers that not all is as it seems. The headmaster is hiding something, and the graduates from the school are never heard from again. Ezra has to confront himself, and decide if he has the strength to be the hero the Academy needs.
